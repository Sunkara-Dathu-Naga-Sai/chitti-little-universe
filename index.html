Dear Sahithi,

My Chitti, my Bunny, my Saas… ❤️

There is a kind of connection I have felt with you that I cannot imagine recreating with anyone else. Your presence brings a kind of peace that is difficult to explain. And when you are not around, even a little, I realise how much your presence means to me.

I still remember that moment in the new city when I couldn't find you. My heartbeat went up, my hands started shaking, and for those few seconds I felt helpless and lost. When I finally found you, the relief and happiness felt like getting a second life. I may never be able to describe all the emotions that passed through me in that one moment.

And then there are your eyes. They are so beautiful that I feel I could look into them for years and still never get tired of them. And your voice — whenever you speak, it feels like the most soothing music. I could keep listening forever. Some things are beautiful not because they need to be explained, but because they make you feel something you cannot put into words.

Then there are the little things I quietly treasure: the way you slowly become comfortable around me, the moment you remove your mask, the way you enjoy your food like a little child, and the way you inspire me without even knowing it. Maybe they look ordinary to everyone else. To me, they are precious.
